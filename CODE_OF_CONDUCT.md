# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

EvalRail welcomes constructive participation. Contributors are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and assume good intent while discussing evidence;
- give specific, actionable technical feedback;
- respect privacy, boundaries, and differing levels of experience;
- accept maintainer decisions about project scope and safety.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not put sensitive details in a public issue. If no suitable private project channel is available and the conduct occurred on GitHub, use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban participants when needed to protect the project and its community. Reports will be considered in context and handled as privately as practical. No response-time guarantee is promised.
